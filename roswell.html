Roswell Slater → William Johnson Slater

Roswell Slater (christened Simsbury, Connecticut, 1774, son of John Slater Jr.) is very probably the father of William Johnson Slater (c.1806–1882). No record found so far names William's father, so the link rests on several independent clues that all point the same way.

Probable, not proven. This is the weakest link in the line.

Against or unresolved

  • In 1880 Asa Slater (b. 1814, Burlington) gave both parents as born in Pennsylvania, and Adolphus gave his mother as born in Pennsylvania. If they were William's full brothers, some of these answers are wrong.
  • William's birth year varies between 1805 and 1809 across records.
  • No land, estate or guardianship record for Roswell or Clarissa appears in the Luzerne, Susquehanna or Wyoming County indexes. Bradford County's deeds and Orphans' Court records are not online.

What would prove it

  • William J. Slater's obituary, July 1882 (Bradford County Historical Society)
  • Bradford County deeds and Orphans' Court records, c.1812–1835 (courthouse, Towanda)
  • Y-DNA comparison with documented descendants of the Simsbury Slaters
