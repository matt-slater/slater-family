The Slater Family of Simsbury and Bradford County

This site traces one paternal line from John Slaughter/Slater, who married at Windsor, Connecticut, in 1669, to Kenneth Harry Slater (1927–1987). Each step is shown with the record that supports it and how strong the evidence is.

The line

  1. John Slaughter (Slater), c.1639–1713. Married Abiah (Gillett) Bartlett at Windsor, CT, 15 July 1669; town clerk of Simsbury.
  2. Deacon John Slater, 1670–1717, Simsbury.
  3. John Slater, 1708–1778, Simsbury; married Abigail Case.
  4. John Slater Jr., 1730–after 1790; Simsbury, then Amenia, Dutchess Co., NY.
  5. Roswell Slater (Slaughter), 1774–c.1830; christened Simsbury; settled Pike/Orwell, Bradford Co., PA.
  6. William Johnson Slater, c.1806–1882 probable link; Pike, Wysox, Auburn, Forkston, Burlington.
  7. Edwin E. Slater, 1845–1931, Burlington Twp.
  8. Harry Grover Slater, 1888–1955, Burlington Twp.
  9. Kenneth Harry Slater, 1927–1987, Methodist minister; died Columbus, Ohio.

Every parent–child link is documented except where marked "probable link". See Roswell → William.

How sure are we?

QuestionCurrent answer
Was John of Windsor the Concord apprentice baptised in Southwark in 1639?Probable, not proven. Seven independent points agree; no record names him in both places.
Was William Johnson Slater a son of Roswell?Probable, not proven. Place, naming, and his father's Connecticut birthplace all fit.
Is the family descended from the Slaughters of Upper Slaughter, Gloucestershire?No evidence. That claim rests on unsourced, merged online trees.
