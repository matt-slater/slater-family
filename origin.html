Where Did John Slater of Simsbury Come From?

John Slaughter/Slater of Windsor and Simsbury, Connecticut (married 1669, died 1713) was very probably the John Slater baptised at St Olave, Southwark, Surrey, on 15 December 1639, son of John Slator, feltmaker. He was brought to Massachusetts as an apprentice by 1656 and lived at Concord until early 1669.

Probable, not proven. No single record names him in both places. The case rests on independent records that agree on name, father's name and trade, county, age, the Slater/Slaughter spelling swap, timing, and possibly handwriting.

The Concord apprentice, 1656–1669

A John Slater, son of a Surrey feltmaker, lived in Concord, Massachusetts, from at least 1656 to January 1668/9. All of these are Middlesex County court papers.

"one John Slater sonne [of] John Slater of Surrey Feltmaker was brought over as an apprentice to be taught a trade and after that the sd John the yonger is sold from one to another without respect to his trade whereby the sd youth is much wronged"

Warrant of Deputy Governor Richard Bellingham to the Concord constable, 23 March 1655/6. FamilySearch film 007831263, image 499.

After January 1668/9 he disappears from Middlesex records. Six months later John Slaughter marries at Windsor.

The Southwark baptism, 1639

The St Olave, Southwark register for December 1639 reads: "John ye sonn of John Slator ffeltmaker ... 15" (London Metropolitan Archives P71/OLA/010). It is the only baptism of a John, son of John Slater, found in Southwark or Surrey between 1634 and 1645. A "John Slator" was buried at St Olave on 10 August 1641; if that was the father, the boy was orphaned before age two.

Evidence against, and loose ends

  • No record names the Concord man as moving to Connecticut.
  • Other John Slaughters lived in Massachusetts (Marblehead, Salem, Suffolk) and are not all excluded.
  • Only Southwark and Surrey registers online were searched; other English candidates can't be ruled out.
  • Handwriting comparison is suggestive at most; the samples are 22 years apart.
